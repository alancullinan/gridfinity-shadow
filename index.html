Gridfinity Tracer

Photograph your items on the printed grid and get a Gridfinity bin with a pocket for each one.

1 Print the template

Print the template at 100% / actual size, then check the 100 mm bar with a ruler. Lay your items flat inside the grid, clear of the four black squares.

Using your own squared paper instead?

Choose "Own paper" in step 2, drag the four corner handles onto the corners of a rectangle you know the size of (for example 36 × 50 squares of 5 mm), and enter its size.

2 Take a photo

Shoot from straight above in soft, even light. Soft, even light works best. Shadows are mostly ignored, but hard, dark shadows can still sneak in, and you can erase them with the touch-up brush. White, very light or cyan items are hard to see on this paper.

3 Design the bin

Layout

Drag items to move them; Shift-drag rotates. Drag a notch's white dot along the edge. Depth zones (dark = deeper, light = shallower): drag to move, square handle to resize.
