Gridfinity Tracer
Step 1 of 3

Print the tracing template

Lay your tools on the printed grid, take one photo, and get a Gridfinity bin with a pocket shaped to each of them.

  1. Print at actual sizeTurn off "fit to page" and set scaling to 100%.
  2. Check the 100 mm barMeasure it with a ruler. If it's off, your printer scaled the page.
  3. Lay out your itemsKeep them inside the grid and clear of the four black squares.
Paper
Using your own squared paper instead?

That works too. In step 2, choose Own paper, drag the four corner handles onto the corners of a rectangle you know the size of (for example 36 × 50 squares of 5 mm) and enter its size. Shadow removal works best with the printed template.
